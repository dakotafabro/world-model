# Your Domain - Failure Modes

## FM-1: Failure Mode Name

**Trigger:** What causes this failure?
**Symptoms:** What does the user/operator see?
**Blast radius:** What else breaks when this happens?
**Detection:** How do you know it's happening? (alerts, dashboards, logs)
**Mitigation:** What do you do immediately?
**Root cause fix:** What prevents it from recurring?
**Last seen:** Date of most recent occurrence, or "theoretical"

## FM-2: Next Failure Mode

Repeat the structure above for each known failure pattern. Source from:
incident post-mortems, on-call runbooks, alert histories, team knowledge.
